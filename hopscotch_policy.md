HOPSCOTCH RETURN AND REFUND POLICY (internal reference, version 2026.1)

1. STANDARD RETURNS (change of mind, wrong size, colour not liked)
- Accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- The item must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached and in its original packaging.
- After 30 days, or if the item has been worn, washed, or has had its tags removed, a standard return is not accepted.

2. MANUFACTURING DEFECTS
- Covered for up to 90 days from delivery, even if the item has been worn or washed according to its care label.
- Examples: seams or stitching coming apart, a sole peeling or separating under normal use, a zip or button failing under normal use, colour bleeding when washed as instructed, a print cracking or peeling under normal washing, elastic losing its stretch, holes or flaws present on arrival.
- Photo evidence is requested before a replacement or refund is issued.
- A defect claim is not affected by the 30-day standard return window.

3. NOT COVERED
- Damage caused by misuse or accidents: pet damage, cuts, stains that appear after use, rough or unintended use.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Damage caused by ignoring the care label, such as washing at a higher temperature than instructed.

4. MANDATORY HUMAN REVIEW
The following must always be reviewed by a human agent and never decided automatically:
- Any child-safety concern (choking hazard, small parts coming loose, injury).
- Any skin reaction or allergy complaint.
- Any order worth more than INR 5,000.
- Any request where the facts are unclear, missing, or conflicting, including cases where it is uncertain whether damage is a defect or misuse.
